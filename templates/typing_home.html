Official SSC CHSL Skill/Typing Test Standards

LDC / JSA Speed Standard

35 Words Per Minute (WPM) in English or 30 WPM in Hindi on Computer. Corresponds to approximately 10,500 key depressions per hour.

Data Entry Operator (DEO)

8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH) on Computer. Passage consists of around 2,000 to 2,200 strokes for a 15-minute test.

Qualifying Nature

The Skill/Typing test is strictly Qualifying in nature. Marks are not added to merit, but passing is mandatory to be eligible for appointment.
